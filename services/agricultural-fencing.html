Frequently Asked Questions

The Answers You Need

What types of agricultural fencing do you install?

We install stock fencing, post and rail, equine fencing, boundary fencing, and more. We work with a range of materials to suit the specific requirements of your land and livestock.

Do you carry out Capital Grants Scheme fencing works?

Yes, we carry out all Capital Grants Scheme fencing works. We're experienced in delivering grant-compliant installations and can advise on eligible works for your holding.

What areas do you cover for agricultural fencing?

We cover counties across the south of the UK, based from Charlbury in Oxfordshire. Larger or more distant projects can also be considered — contact us to discuss your location.

How long does an agricultural fencing project take to complete?

Timescales vary depending on the scale and terrain, but most farm fencing projects are completed efficiently within the agreed schedule. We'll give you a realistic timeframe when we visit to quote.

What materials do you use for agricultural fencing?

We use quality, commercially-proven materials suited to agricultural environments. Timber posts, stock wire, plain and barbed wire, and rail fencing are all available depending on what the project calls for.
