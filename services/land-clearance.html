Efficient Land Clearance Solutions

TMB carries out land clearance and site preparation across the south of the UK, tackling overgrown vegetation, scrub, and hedges to restore land to a usable state. Whether you're reclaiming agricultural land or preparing a site for development, we have the equipment and experience to get the job done.

We handle everything from light scrub removal to heavy vegetation clearance, working safely and efficiently on all types of terrain. Our team assesses the site before starting to ensure the right approach is taken and the work is completed in a way that's practical for the landowner.

On completion, the site is left clear, accessible, and ready for whatever comes next. We work alongside other contractors where needed and can coordinate with follow-on trades to keep your project moving forward on schedule.
